## Title
chore(design-system): align PageHeaderAside to bottom of text content

## State

## Body
## What kind of change does this PR introduce?

UI update.

## What is the current behavior?

`PageHeaderAside` is aligned to the middle of the content to its left due to the parent having `items-center` applied. This makes its buttons look floating when a description is applied.

## What is the new behavior?

- `items-end` on parent
- `text-box-trim: trim-end;` applied to text elements to reduce extraneous white space
