## Body
## What kind of change does this PR introduce?

Component clean up.

## What is the current behavior?

- `AlertError` is composed of Shadcn primitives instead of our fit-for-purpose `Admonition`
	- This results in some errors/warnings looking different to others and component confusion
- `Admonition` has an opinionated `mb-2` margin despite being used in all sorts of ways

## What is the new behavior?

- `AlertError` uses `Admonition` under the hood
	- Logic added to handle `layout`
